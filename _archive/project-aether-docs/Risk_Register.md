# Risk Register: Project Aether

| Risk ID | Category | Risk Description | Prob (1-5) | Imp (1-5) | Score (PxI) | Early Warning Indicators | Mitigation Strategy | Contingency Plan | Owner |
|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **RSK-001** | Technical | **ControlNet API Incompatibility:** Commercial APIs (Veo, Kling, Runway) do not accept raw depth/normal tensors. | 5 | 4 | **20** | Provider docs show only image/text endpoints. | Use UE5 to render photorealistic First/Last keyframe pairs; interpolate via closed APIs. | Route Level 4/5 shots to local/cloud open-weights models (CogVideoX) with native ControlNet support. | Compiler Lead |
| **RSK-002** | Technical | **VLM Temporal Blindspot:** Multimodal LLMs fail to detect micro-flickers or rapid 3-frame morphing defects. | 4 | 5 | **20** | High false-negative rate in AetherBench temporal defect tests. | Combine OpenCV optical flow and frame-to-frame SSIM variance with VLM semantic auditing. | Fallback to optical-flow threshold gating before passing video to VLM. | Quality Lead |
| **RSK-003** | Financial | **Tournament & Inference Cost Explosion:** Generating multi-model video candidates and VLM evaluations drains API tokens and GPU budget. | 4 | 4 | **16** | Daily API token/credit consumption spikes during test runs. | Enforce TeaCache/PAB acceleration (65-80% GPU savings), VLM Context Caching (90% token discount), Speculative 480p draft gating, and Dream-RSI offline dreaming. | Enforce strict per-scene credit caps, fallback to 4-step local distilled flow models. | Ops / Teacher Lead |
| **RSK-004** | Technical | **Temporal Inpainting Boundary Seams:** Localized inpainting creates visible boundary seams or temporal strobing. | 4 | 4 | **16** | Critic Council flags bounding-box borders during repair verification. | Use latent feathering and optical-flow guided blend masks for seamless edge integration. | Widen repair bounding box to full object or fall back to complete shot re-generation. | Repair Lead |
| **RSK-005** | Operational | **API Deprecation & Parameter Drift:** Cloud video generation providers alter endpoints, latency, or model weights without notice. | 3 | 4 | **12** | Unexpected 4xx/5xx API responses or sudden drop in empirical quality scores. | Abstract all model communication behind strict adapter interfaces; run daily canary tests. | Route traffic automatically to alternative model families in the Model Tournament. | Integration Lead |
| **RSK-006** | Infrastructure | **Headless 3D Render Cold Starts:** Launching headless Unreal Engine 5 instances introduces long initialization latency. | 3 | 3 | **9** | Shot compilation latency exceeds 60 seconds per shot. | Maintain a warm worker pool of headless Blender/UE5 daemon processes. | Use lightweight procedural Python geometry (PyMesh/Trimesh) for Level 3/4 blocking. | Infra Lead |
| **RSK-007** | Quality | **Acoustic / Visual Desync:** Generated speech or Foley cues drift out of temporal phase with visual events. | 3 | 4 | **12** | Lip-sync critic fails hard gate on dialogue scenes. | Lock all audio triggers to exact mathematical video timestamps; synthesize audio to match video duration before muxing. | Apply automated audio retime warping and dedicated Wav2Lip post-processing. | Audio Lead |
| **RSK-008** | Technical | **Replay Simulator Distribution Drift:** Historical discovery trees become outdated when commercial model endpoints upgrade or drift. | 3 | 3 | **9** | Discrepancy between simulated dream score and real online render performance. | Continual online rollout pool expansion (Dream-RSI Stage 1); prune discovery trees older than 60 days. | Trigger targeted canary calibration batch on AetherBench. | Teacher Lead |
